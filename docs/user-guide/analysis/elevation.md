# Elevation

Colours the terrain by **height**.

## Settings

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Colour ramp** | Colours the terrain preview by elevation. Drag the ramp's stops, the band interval, or the mapped range |

## Results

The **Area** of the terrain surface from the last build.

## Tips

Use a stepped ramp with the interval matching your contour interval for a classic hypsometric tint.
