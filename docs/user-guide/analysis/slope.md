# Slope

Colours each face of the terrain by its **steepness**.

## Settings

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Units** | The unit the ramp range and results are written in (percent, promille, ratio or degrees); the range converts when you switch |
| **Colour ramp** | Colours the terrain preview by slope. Drag the ramp's stops, the band interval, or the mapped range |

## Results

**Min / Avg / Max** slope from the last build, in the card's unit.

## Tips

- Use bands (a stepped ramp) with limits from your design brief, for example green below 1:20, amber to
  1:12, red above.
- Add a [Legend](../annotations/legend.md) to key the colours on a drawing.
